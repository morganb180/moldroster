# How we verify listings

Every listing comes from a state licensing agency's own public records. Nothing is added from reviews or scraped directories.

## Sources and last retrieval

* **Texas:** Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation ([source](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/mold/mold.htm)), retrieved 2026-10-12.
* **Florida:** Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation ([source](https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/mold-related-services/)), retrieved 2026-10-12.
* **New York:** New York State Department of Labor ([source](https://dol.ny.gov/mold-program)), retrieved 2026-10-12.
* **Washington, DC:** DC Department of Energy & Environment ([source](https://doee.dc.gov/service/mold-professional-licensing)), retrieved 2026-10-12.

## What we check

* The licence appears on the agency record, and the licence number and expiry date (where published) are shown on the listing with a link to the source.
* Records are grouped by firm or shared address; individuals without a published address are flagged.

## What we don't verify

* Workmanship, prices, insurance or availability.
* Contact details a pro adds later, until the claim process checks them against the licence record.

## Corrections

Use [claim, correct or remove a listing](https://moldroster.com/claim-listing/). See also [about our data](https://moldroster.com/about-our-data/).
