Mold remediation in Texas: licences, rules and who to hire

Reviewed by Mold Roster staff · Last updated November 1, 2026

Quick answer: In Texas, mold assessment and remediation are licensed by TDLR. A licence is generally needed when the mold covers 25 contiguous square feet or more. The assessor writes a protocol, a different licensed company does the removal, and the assessor checks the result before a Certificate of Mold Damage Remediation is issued.

Who needs a Texas mold licence?

Anyone who does mold assessment or remediation for others generally needs a TDLR licence, unless an exemption in Occupations Code Chapter 1958 applies.

  • Remediation of less than 25 contiguous square feet of visible mold doesn't need a licence (Occupations Code § 1958.102).
  • Owners and tenants working on their own property are exempt unless the area is 25 contiguous square feet or more. TDLR's consumer sheet says homeowners may sample or clean their own home.
  • An owner of residential property with fewer than 10 dwelling units is exempt for that property (§ 1958.102).
  • Remediation of 25 contiguous square feet or more needs a notification to TDLR, filed online with a $25 fee (TDLR notification requirements).

Can the same company assess and remove mold in Texas?

No. A licence holder may not do both the assessment and the remediation on the same project (§ 1958.155).

That's why every Texas listing on Mold Roster shows which licence it holds. Hire an independent assessor first, then a licensed remediator. See assessment vs remediation.

What paperwork should I get?

A written protocol from the assessor, a work plan from the remediator, a passed clearance report, and a Certificate of Mold Damage Remediation within 10 days of a passed clearance.

  • Protocol: rooms or areas, quantities, methods and clearance criteria (§ 1958.151).
  • Work plan: how the remediator will carry out the protocol; a copy stays on site (§ 1958.152).
  • Certificate of Mold Damage Remediation: signed by the remediator and the assessor, and states whether the underlying cause was corrected (§ 1958.154; TDLR consumer sheet).
  • Licensees carry commercial general liability insurance of at least $1 million, or are self-insured (TDLR consumer sheet).

Why does the certificate matter later?

TDLR's consumer sheet says the certificate prevents an insurer from making an underwriting decision based on previous mold damage, and Texas sellers must give buyers certificates issued in the previous 5 years.

Keep it with your home records. See mold disclosure in real estate and mold and insurance.

How do I check a Texas mold licence?

Ask for the licence number in writing and check it with TDLR.

TDLR's mold program: Mold Assessors and Remediators. Each Mold Roster listing shows the licence numbers and expiry dates from TDLR's file.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a licence to clean mold in my own Texas home?

No. TDLR's consumer sheet says homeowners may sample or clean their own home. Owners of residential property with fewer than 10 units are exempt for that property.

What is the 25 square foot rule in Texas?

Remediation of less than 25 contiguous square feet of visible mold doesn't require a licence, and remediation of 25 contiguous square feet or more requires notifying TDLR.

Who issues the Certificate of Mold Damage Remediation?

It is signed by the licensed remediator and the licensed assessor after a passed clearance, within 10 days, and states whether the underlying cause was corrected.
