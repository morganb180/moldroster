# Mold disclosure in real estate: Texas, New York and DC

Reviewed by Mold Roster staff · Last updated November 1, 2026

**Quick answer:** Texas sellers must give buyers copies of any Certificate of Mold Damage Remediation issued in the previous 5 years. New York's property condition disclosure statement asks whether the home has been tested for indoor mold. DC landlords must disclose known mold from the previous 3 years to prospective tenants unless a licensed pro remediated it.

## What must Texas sellers disclose about mold?

Texas sellers must give buyers copies of every Certificate of Mold Damage Remediation issued for the property in the previous 5 years (Occupations Code § 1958.154).

* The statutory seller's disclosure notice (Property Code § 5.008) doesn't use the word mold, but asks about previous water damage not due to a flood, flood water penetration, and conditions that materially affect health or safety.
* If the notice is delivered late, the buyer may terminate within 7 days (§ 5.008).
* Some sales are exempt, such as foreclosures.

## What must New York sellers disclose about mold?

New York's property condition disclosure statement asks whether the property has been tested for indoor mold, and to attach the report (Real Property Law § 462, item 27).

* Other items ask about rot or water damage, water seepage or flooding, and flood-zone and flood-insurance history.
* The statement is delivered before the buyer signs a binding contract, for 1–4 family homes.

## What must DC sellers and landlords disclose?

DC sellers give a written disclosure statement before or when the purchase agreement is signed; if it's late, the buyer may terminate within 5 calendar days (D.C. Code § 42-1302).

* DC landlords must disclose known indoor mold contamination from the previous 3 years to prospective tenants, unless a licensed professional remediated it (§ 42-3502.22).
* See [DC mold rules](https://moldroster.com/mold-assessment-dc/).

## What about Florida?

This page doesn't cover Florida yet. We haven't found a Florida statute on mold disclosure that we could cite, so we don't summarise one.

## What should a buyer do about mold?

Ask for any mold reports and remediation certificates, and consider a licensed mold assessment during the option or inspection period.

* A Texas certificate shows whether the underlying cause was corrected.
* Use an assessor who is independent of any remediation company.
* [What a mold inspection costs](https://moldroster.com/mold-inspection-cost/).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a Texas seller have to disclose past mold remediation?**

Yes, through certificates: sellers must give buyers copies of Certificates of Mold Damage Remediation issued in the previous 5 years.

**Does New York require mold disclosure?**

The property condition disclosure statement asks whether the property has been tested for indoor mold and to attach the report.

**Do DC landlords have to tell tenants about mold?**

Yes, for known indoor mold contamination in the previous 3 years, unless a licensed professional remediated it.
