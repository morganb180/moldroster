# Does homeowners insurance cover mold?

Reviewed by Mold Roster staff · Last updated November 1, 2026

**Quick answer:** Often only when the mold comes from a covered event. The Texas Department of Insurance says most home policies won't pay for mold removal except to repair damage from a covered risk, and don't cover a continuous leak. Standard NFIP flood policies don't cover mold damage, with limited exceptions.

## When does home insurance pay for mold?

TDI says most Texas home policies won't cover mold removal except to repair damage caused by a covered risk, and won't cover a continuous water leak. Mold coverage can be bought as an option.

* Read your policy's water damage and mold sections, and ask your agent what is excluded.
* Slow, continuous leaks are commonly excluded (TDI).

## Does flood insurance cover mold?

Standard NFIP flood policies don't cover mold damage. FloodSmart says a policy may cover it if officials restricted access to your home, or floodwater stayed in or around it so you couldn't clean up.

* FloodSmart: mold can start forming within 24–48 hours of flooding.
* Photograph damage before cleanup, keep samples of damaged materials and keep receipts.
* See [mold after flooding](https://moldroster.com/mold-after-flooding/).

## What is assignment of benefits in Florida?

An assignment of benefits signs your insurance claim over to a contractor. For Florida homeowners policies issued on or after January 1, 2023, assignment of post-loss benefits is void (§ 627.7152).

* For policies issued from July 1, 2019 to before January 1, 2023, the statute's assignment rules apply, including a 14-day right to rescind.
* Read anything a contractor asks you to sign about your claim.

## How can a Texas remediation certificate help with insurance?

TDLR says the Certificate of Mold Damage Remediation prevents an insurer from making an underwriting decision based on previous mold damage.

Keep the certificate. See [mold remediation in Texas](https://moldroster.com/mold-remediation-texas/).

## How do I document a mold claim?

Photograph everything before cleanup, keep samples and receipts, get written estimates and use licensed, verified contractors.

* FloodSmart advises photos before cleanup, keeping samples and receipts, and checking contractors' qualifications and references.
* Ask for a written scope that separates moisture repair from mold removal.
* Use a licensed assessor who doesn't do the removal (required in Texas and New York, and within 12 months in Florida).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is mold from a slow leak covered?**

Usually not. TDI says most Texas home policies don't cover a continuous water leak.

**Does NFIP flood insurance cover mold?**

Not under standard policies, except in limited cases such as when officials restricted access or floodwater stayed around the home.

**Can a Florida contractor take over my claim?**

Not for policies issued on or after January 1, 2023: Florida law voids assignment of post-loss benefits for those policies.
