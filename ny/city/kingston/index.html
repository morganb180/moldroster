Frequently asked questions

How do I check a mold pro's licence in Kingston?

Look the name up in the NYS DOL records and ask for the licence number in writing. Each listing here shows the licence numbers from the agency's records. The Kingston hiring guide lists questions to ask.

How much does mold inspection cost in Kingston?

National HomeAdvisor figures put mold inspection or assessment at $303–$1,075 (average about $689) and mold remediation at $1,223–$3,757 (average about $2,368). There are no Kingston-specific prices, so get written quotes. See the cost guide and estimate tool.

Can the same company test and remove mold in Kingston?

No. In New York, the company that assesses your mold can't also remove it on the same job, so get assessment and remediation quotes from different licensed firms.

Our advice

What should I check before hiring a mold pro in Kingston?

  1. Look the name up in the official NYS DOL records and ask for the licence number in writing.
  2. Use separate, independent firms for assessment and for remediation: in New York, the company that assesses your mold can't also remove it on the same job.
  3. Get proof of insurance and a written scope before work starts, and a post-remediation check by the assessor.
