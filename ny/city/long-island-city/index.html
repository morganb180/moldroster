How much does mold inspection cost in Long Island City?

Can the same company test and remove mold in Long Island City?

No. In New York, the company that assesses your mold can't also remove it on the same job, so get assessment and remediation quotes from different licensed firms.

Our advice

What should I check before hiring a mold pro in Long Island City?

  1. Look the name up in the official NYS DOL records and ask for the licence number in writing.
  2. Use separate, independent firms for assessment and for remediation: in New York, the company that assesses your mold can't also remove it on the same job.
  3. Get proof of insurance and a written scope before work starts, and a post-remediation check by the assessor.
