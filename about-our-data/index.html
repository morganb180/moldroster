About our data

  • Texas: TDLR licence data files (companies, consultants, contractors), retrieved 2026-10-12. Current licences only. Individual files give name and county only.
  • Florida: DBPR mold-related services public-records file lic07mold.csv, retrieved 2026-10-12. Current/active licences only. Licensees are grouped by DBA name or shared street address.
  • New York: NYS DOL mold contractor licences (data.ny.gov ikqx-ispy), retrieved 2026-10-12. Active licences only.
  • DC: DOEE list of licensed mold professionals, retrieved 2026-10-12. Grouped by email domain or company. No licence numbers published.
  • Cleanup: expired/inactive licences dropped; names and addresses case-normalized; phones formatted; counties from ZIP (GeoNames, CC BY 4.0). Every change is in data/cleanup-log.csv.
  • Added from Google Maps: For 1,021 licensed firms that we matched with high confidence to their Google Maps listing (October 30, 2026; matched by the state-listed phone, or by name plus the same street or ZIP), cards show the Google rating from that listing, labelled with its source and date. Phone, email and the firm's website are not published. Reaching a firm is the quote connection: we connect you with up to 3 nearby licensed pros, and they contact you directly. The lead router keeps its own contact list; these pages do not display it. Individual licensees and firms without a confident match show only the state data.
  • Not covered: Louisiana (LSLBC bulk roster is a paid order).

How this directory works

Where listings come from

Public licence records from Texas TDLR, Florida DBPR, NYS Department of Labor and DC DOEE, retrieved Oct 12, 2026. ZIP-to-county matching uses GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Not affiliated with those agencies; a listing is not an endorsement.

How they're ordered

By location and name. Firms can't pay to be ranked higher. Any paid placement is labeled "Ad" and sits apart from the free list.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the licence number and open the state record linked on the listing. See How we verify.
