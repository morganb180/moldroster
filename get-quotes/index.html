How to get quotes from licensed mold pros

Mold Roster doesn't take quote requests online yet. The fastest route is to contact 2-3 licensed pros yourself: every listing shows the phone, email or website from the official record.

How do I get quotes from licensed mold pros?

Find your area, contact 2–3 licensed pros who serve your address, and compare written quotes after checking each licence number against the state record.

  1. Find your area. Search above or choose your state. County and city pages list every licensee with an address there.
  2. Contact 2-3 pros. Ask whether they serve your address, and when they can come out. In Texas, Florida and New York the company that assesses your mold can't also remove it on the same job, so get assessment and remediation quotes from different firms.
  3. Compare written quotes. Check each licence number against the state record (linked on every listing).

What should I ask a mold pro before hiring?

Ask for the licence number and type, whether an independent assessor will write the protocol and check clearance, whether the moisture source will be fixed, how the area will be contained and what the written scope includes.

  • What is your state licence number, and is it an assessor or remediator licence?
  • Will an independent licensed assessor write the protocol and do the clearance check?
  • Will you find and fix the moisture source, or only remove visible growth?
  • How will you contain the work area and protect the rest of the house?
  • What does the written scope include: demolition, HEPA cleaning, disposal, rebuild?
  • Do you carry liability insurance that covers mold work?
  • Will you work with my insurance adjuster, and do you need a signed assignment of benefits? (Read it carefully first.)
  • What clearance standard or test will show the job is done?
