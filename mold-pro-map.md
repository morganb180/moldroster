# Map: licensed mold pros by county in Texas, Florida and New York

Reviewed by Mold Roster staff · Last updated November 1, 2026

County maps of licensed mold pro listings per 100,000 residents. Data: [mold-licence-data-2026.csv](https://moldroster.com/mold-license-data-report-2026/mold-licence-data-2026.csv). Report: https://moldroster.com/mold-license-data-report-2026/

* Texas: 2,192 listings, 7.0 per 100,000 residents; 102 of 254 counties have at least one.
* Florida: 3,849 listings, 16.5 per 100,000 residents; 57 of 67 counties have at least one.
* New York: 1,382 listings, 7.0 per 100,000 residents; 52 of 62 counties have at least one.
