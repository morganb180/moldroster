# Mold remediation in Florida: licences, rules and who to hire

Reviewed by Mold Roster staff · Last updated November 1, 2026

**Quick answer:** Florida licenses mold assessors and mold remediators through DBPR under Chapter 468, Part XVI. The licensed work covers mold areas over 10 square feet. A company may not remediate a building it assessed in the previous 12 months, or assess one it remediated.

## Who needs a Florida mold licence?

Mold assessment and mold remediation of areas over 10 square feet are licensed work in Florida (§ 468.8411).

* Licensees pass an exam and document training in water, mold and respiratory protection (§ 468.8414).
* A mold remediator can't do work that needs a Chapter 489 contractor licence unless licensed for it (§ 468.8411).

## Can the same company assess and remove mold in Florida?

Not on the same building within 12 months. An assessor may not remediate a structure it assessed in the previous 12 months, and a remediator may not assess one it remediated (§ 468.8419).

* Exception: a Division I contractor may do both, but must disclose your right to get competitive bids (§ 468.8419).
* Assessors may not inspect where they have a financial interest, pay or take referral fees, or charge fees that depend on the result (§ 468.8419).

## What insurance must Florida mold pros carry?

Assessors carry general liability and errors-and-omissions insurance of at least $1 million; remediators carry general liability of at least $1 million that covers mold-related claims (§ 468.8421).

## How many licensed mold pros are in Florida?

DBPR's file, retrieved October 12, 2026, gives us 4,161 Florida listings covering 4,359 individual licensees.

* 2,982 listings hold an assessment licence and 2,917 hold a remediation licence.
* Listings have an address of record in 57 of Florida's 67 counties.
* Every licence in the file expires on July 31, 2028.

## How do I check a Florida mold licence?

Ask for the licence number (it starts with MRSA for assessors or MRSR for remediators in DBPR's file) and look it up with DBPR.

[DBPR Mold-Related Services](https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/mold-related-services/).

## What about insurance and assignment of benefits in Florida?

For homeowners policies issued on or after January 1, 2023, Florida law voids assignment of post-loss benefits (§ 627.7152).

## Frequently asked questions

**What size of mold job needs a licensed pro in Florida?**

Florida's definitions of mold assessment and remediation cover areas over 10 square feet (§ 468.8411).

**Can a Florida mold inspector also do the removal?**

Not on a building it assessed in the previous 12 months, unless it is a Division I contractor that discloses your right to competitive bids (§ 468.8419).

**Why can't I request quotes in Florida?**

DBPR's public licence file has no phone numbers or email addresses, so Mold Roster can't send requests to Florida pros yet.
