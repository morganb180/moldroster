## How listing links work

Every listing has its own page, so the badge links straight to it:

* **Listing pages:** https://moldroster.com/<state>/firms/<name>-<listing-id>/, for example [https://moldroster.com/tx/firms/burcham-environmental-services-llc-tx0001/](https://moldroster.com/tx/firms/burcham-environmental-services-llc-tx0001/). The code generator fills it in.
* The listing ID is the market code plus a number (for example TX0001). It appears in your listing page address.
* Badge files are named /badges/<market>-licensed-<type>-<style>.svg. Markets: tx, fl, ny, dc. Types: professional, assessment, remediation, assessment-remediation. Styles: seal, light, dark, compact.

## Badge rules

* **What it says:** your listing appears in the state agency's mold licence records for the type shown, as of the list date printed on the badge. Clicking it takes people to your Mold Roster listing, so they can check the licence details themselves.
* **Who licenses:** mold licences are issued only by the state agency (TDLR in Texas, DBPR in Florida, the NYS Department of Labor in New York, DOEE in DC). Mold Roster does not license, certify, inspect or endorse anyone. The badge is not issued, approved or endorsed by any agency, and Mold Roster is not affiliated with them.
* **Pick an accurate type.** "Mold Professional" fits any listed licence. Assessment and Remediation must match a licence you hold. The code generator only offers types on the agency record for your listing.
* **Only while licensed.** Use the badge only while your licence is current. If it lapses or expires, you must remove the badge.
* Free and optional. It does not change your listing order. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment.
* Don't modify the artwork or wording, combine it with government seals or logos, or describe it as "state-approved" or "Mold Roster certified". You may scale it. Keep the seal at least 120 px wide.
