# Where are the licensed mold pros? 2026 mold licence data report

Reviewed by Mold Roster staff · Last updated November 1, 2026

We counted every licensed mold assessor and remediator in the official records for Texas, Florida, New York and Washington, DC, and matched them to counties and Census population. Here is where they are, where there are none, and how many licences expire soon.

## Key findings

* **Texas:** 2,192 listings with an in-state address of record, about 7.0 per 100,000 residents. Harris County has the most (445); the top 5 counties hold 50% of the state's listings.
* **Florida:** 3,849 listings with an in-state address of record, about 16.5 per 100,000 residents. Broward County has the most (589); the top 5 counties hold 50% of the state's listings.
* **New York:** 1,382 listings with an in-state address of record, about 7.0 per 100,000 residents. Suffolk County has the most (185); the top 5 counties hold 52% of the state's listings.
* **152 of 254 Texas counties** have no licensed mold pro listing with an address there (1,887,224 residents in those counties). Pros often travel, so this doesn't mean no one serves them.
* **10 of 67 Florida counties** have no licensed mold pro listing with an address there (159,111 residents in those counties). Pros often travel, so this doesn't mean no one serves them.
* **10 of 62 New York counties** have no licensed mold pro listing with an address there (357,018 residents in those counties). Pros often travel, so this doesn't mean no one serves them.
* **Expiring soon:** 173 Texas licences (of 2,439 with an expiry date) and 138 New York licences (of 1,748) expire before January 1, 2027. All 6,126 Florida licences in DBPR's file expire on July 31, 2028.
* **Companies vs individuals:** in Texas, 1,696 of 2,357 listings are individual licensees rather than companies; in Florida, DBPR's file names 4,359 licensed individuals, and only 72 Florida listings carry a company name. New York's file lists businesses only (1,540).

## Methodology

* Listings are Mold Roster's directory records built from the official licence files: TDLR (Texas), DBPR (Florida), NYS DOL via data.ny.gov (New York) and DOEE (DC), all retrieved October 12, 2026.
* County counts use each listing's address of record. Listings with an out-of-state address are counted in the market totals but not in any county. 9 Texas listings, 3 New York listings had no county and are left out of county counts.
* Per-100,000 figures divide listings by the Census Bureau's July 1, 2024 county population estimates (Vintage 2024). Small counties swing widely, so per-resident rankings only include counties of 100,000 or more.
* A listing is a company or an individual licensee, as the agency publishes it. Florida individuals at the same street address are grouped into one listing. Where a pro works isn't recorded; many travel.
* Nothing here is a ranking of quality. Licence status can change after the retrieval date; confirm with the agency.
