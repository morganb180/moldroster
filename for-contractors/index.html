For mold contractors

Every licensed mold assessor and remediator in the records we use already has a free listing here: 8,203 listings built from the public licence files of TDLR (Texas), DBPR (Florida), the NYS Department of Labor (New York) and DOEE (DC). Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment.

What you can do now

  • Claim your listing to add a website or service area, correct details, or ask us to remove it.
  • Get a free licence badge for your website. It shows your state agency and licence type and links to your listing so homeowners can verify it.
  • Check that your licence is current: we only list licensees on the agency's records.

Quote requests

In Texas, New York and Washington, DC, homeowners can ask for quotes from up to 3 nearby licensed pros that offer the service they need, closest first. Pros can't pay to be picked. Florida requests aren't routed yet because DBPR's file has no contact details.

If we offer paid options for contractors in the future, they will be labeled, and they will never change the free list or the badge. See advertising & pricing and how we make money.
