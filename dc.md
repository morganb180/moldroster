# Mold assessors and remediators licensed in Washington, DC

**145 DOEE-licensed listings** (79 mold assessment, 79 mold remediation). DC licenses individual mold assessors and remediators. DOEE's public list gives name, company, phone and email but no licence numbers or addresses.

## What are Washington, DC's mold licensing rules?

If there is 10 square feet or more of indoor mold, assessment and remediation must be done or supervised by a DOEE-licensed professional (DOEE).

* If there is 10 square feet or more of indoor mold, assessment and remediation must be done or supervised by a DOEE-licensed professional (DOEE).
* DOEE issues separate two-year licences for mold assessment and for mold remediation.
* Since June 2026 DOEE no longer does residential mold complaint inspections; housing code complaints go to the Department of Buildings (DOEE).

Summary of D.C. Code §§ 8-241.01-8-241.09 and 20 DCMR Chapter 32. See the agency: [DC Department of Energy & Environment](https://doee.dc.gov/service/mold-information-tenants). Plain-language summary, not legal advice; confirm details with the agency.

DOEE's list does not give business addresses, so listings are grouped by the state DOEE records for the licensee (blank = not stated).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a licensed mold pro in Washington, DC?**

If there is 10 square feet or more of indoor mold, assessment and remediation must be done or supervised by a DOEE-licensed professional (DOEE).

**How many licensed mold pros are in Washington, DC?**

Mold Roster lists 145 DOEE-licensed listings in Washington, DC: 79 with an assessment licence and 79 with a remediation licence (records retrieved 2026-10-12).

**Can the same company test and remove the mold?**

DOEE licenses the two roles separately. Using an independent assessor is the safer choice; ask DOEE about any conflict-of-interest rules.

**How much does mold remediation cost?**

A national cost guide puts typical remediation at $1,223-$3,758 (average about $2,369) and inspections at $303-$1,075. Get written quotes.
