Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026. This policy covers Mold Roster (moldroster.com, "we").

What we collect

  • Listing requests and messages: the listing request form opens your own email app; we receive only what you send us by email, and use it to verify and process the request.
  • Server logs: our host keeps standard request logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and reliability.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect aggregate usage statistics (pages viewed, the place searches you enter, how the search and quote or listing forms are used, and which listing links are clicked) so we can improve the site. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies and receives your IP address and browser details; we never send it your name, email, phone, address or anything you type into a form field. We have turned off Google signals and ad personalization, and we don't use it for advertising. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, Google Analytics doesn't load at all. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. We don't use advertising cookies.

Business listings

Listings come from public licensing or certification records (see About our data). Professionals can claim, correct or ask to remove their listing at any time.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't share your messages except with service providers that host the site and deliver email.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to delete it, email hello@moldroster.com.

Changes

We'll post changes here and update the date above.

Disclaimer: Listings are compiled from public licence records: Texas TDLR licence data files, Florida DBPR mold-related services public records, NYS Department of Labor mold contractor licences (data.ny.gov) and DC DOEE's list of licensed mold professionals, retrieved 2026-10-12. ZIP-to-county matching uses GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Inclusion is not an endorsement, and Mold Roster is not affiliated with any licensing agency. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment; any paid placement will be labeled "Ad". Always verify a licence with the issuing agency before hiring.
