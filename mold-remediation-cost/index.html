How much does mold inspection and remediation cost?

National HomeAdvisor figures put mold inspection or assessment at $303–$1,075 (average about $689) and mold remediation at $1,223–$3,757 (average about $2,368). These are not local quotes.

What affects the cost of mold remediation?

The size and location of the affected area, whether drywall or other materials must come out, lab sampling and the post-remediation check, and moisture repairs, which are often priced separately.

  • The size and location of the affected area (attic, crawl space, HVAC, wall cavities).
  • Whether drywall or other materials must be removed.
  • Lab sampling and the post-remediation check.
  • Moisture repairs, which are often priced separately.
