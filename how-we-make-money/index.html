How we make money

Mold Roster is free to use. Here is how it is, and will be, paid for, and the rules we hold ourselves to.

Today

  • Nothing is for sale. Every listing is free and built from official public records.
  • Listings are ordered by location and name. No one can pay to appear higher, to get a license badge, or to have a competitor removed.

Planned

  • Featured placements, clearly labeled "Ad", shown alongside, never instead of, the full free list.
  • Quote requests routed to licensed pros near you, if we add them, will be free for homeowners. Pros may pay a per-request fee; that will never change who is listed or the order of the free list.

Our rules

  • Paid or not, every listing must match a current official record.
  • Guides and cost pages are written independently of advertisers and cite their sources.
  • If we ever earn a referral fee from a link, the link will say so.

Professionals: see claim or correct your listing.

Disclaimer: Listings are compiled from public licence records: Texas TDLR licence data files, Florida DBPR mold-related services public records, NYS Department of Labor mold contractor licences (data.ny.gov) and DC DOEE's list of licensed mold professionals, retrieved 2026-10-12. ZIP-to-county matching uses GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Inclusion is not an endorsement, and Mold Roster is not affiliated with any licensing agency. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment; any paid placement will be labeled "Ad". Always verify a licence with the issuing agency before hiring.
