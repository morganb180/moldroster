For professionals

Every current licensee on the TDLR, DBPR, NYS DOL, DOEE lists gets a free listing on Mold Roster, built from the official record and linked to it.

Free listing

  • Shown on your county and city pages, ordered by location and name.
  • Shows your license numbers and a link to the state record, so homeowners can check you.
  • Claim it to add a phone number, website or service area, or to fix an error.

Featured placement

Not available yet. When it is, featured spots will be labeled "Ad", limited per county, open only to current licensees, and shown alongside the full free list, never instead of it. To hear when it opens, send an advertising request.

Disclaimer: Listings are compiled from public licence records: Texas TDLR licence data files, Florida DBPR mold-related services public records, NYS Department of Labor mold contractor licences (data.ny.gov) and DC DOEE's list of licensed mold professionals, retrieved 2026-10-12. ZIP-to-county matching uses GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Inclusion is not an endorsement, and Mold Roster is not affiliated with any licensing agency. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment; any paid placement will be labeled "Ad". Always verify a licence with the issuing agency before hiring.
