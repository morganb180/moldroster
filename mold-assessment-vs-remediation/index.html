Mold assessment vs mold remediation

Assessment means inspecting for mold and moisture, sampling where useful, and writing a remediation protocol. Remediation means removing and cleaning up the mold under that protocol. Texas, Florida, New York and DC license the two roles separately, and Texas, Florida and New York stop one company from doing both on the same job, so the firm that finds the problem isn't the one paid to fix it.

What is the typical sequence for mold assessment and remediation?

Fix or identify the moisture source, a licensed assessor inspects and writes the protocol, a licensed remediator does the work with containment, then an independent assessor does a post-remediation assessment.

  1. Fix or identify the moisture source (EPA: A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture and Your Home).
  2. A licensed assessor inspects and writes the protocol.
  3. A licensed remediator does the work, with containment.
  4. The assessor (or another independent one) does a post-remediation assessment.

How much does mold inspection and remediation cost?

National HomeAdvisor figures put mold inspection or assessment at $303–$1,075 (average about $689) and mold remediation at $1,223–$3,757 (average about $2,368). These are not local quotes.
