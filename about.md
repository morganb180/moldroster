# About Mold Roster

Mold Roster is a free directory of state-licensed mold assessors and remediators in Texas, Florida, New York and Washington DC, built from official licence records.

Mold Roster is for homeowners, tenants, landlords, buyers and insurance claimants who need a licensed mold assessor (inspection/testing) or remediator (removal).

## Where the information comes from

* **Texas:** Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation ([official list](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/mld/))
* **Florida:** Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation ([official list](https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/mold-related-services/))
* **New York:** New York State Department of Labor ([official list](https://dol.ny.gov/mold-program))
* **Washington, DC:** DC Department of Energy & Environment ([official list](https://doee.dc.gov/service/mold-professional-licensing))

8,203 listings, grouped by state, county and city. Guides: [Mold assessment vs remediation](https://moldroster.com/mold-assessment-vs-remediation/), [Mold inspection & remediation cost + estimate tool](https://moldroster.com/mold-remediation-cost/), [Mold licensing rules by state](https://moldroster.com/mold-licensing-by-state/), [How to hire a mold pro](https://moldroster.com/hiring-a-mold-pro/).

## How it's built

* Listings are rebuilt from the official source files on each refresh; every listing shows its source and retrieval date.
* Rules and cost guides link the statute, regulation, agency page or official fee schedule they rely on. Where we couldn't confirm something from an official source, we say so or leave it out.
* Nothing here is legal advice. Requirements change, so confirm with the agency or town before you act.

## Who operates Mold Roster

Mold Roster (moldroster.com) is operated by Mold Roster. Contact us through the [contact page](https://moldroster.com/contact/).

## Independence

Mold Roster is independent. It isn't affiliated with any government agency, licensing board or contractor, and a listing is not an endorsement.
