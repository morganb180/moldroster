Frequently asked questions

How do I check a mold pro's licence in Boca Raton?

Look the name up in the FL DBPR records and ask for the licence number in writing. Each listing here shows the licence numbers from the agency's records. The Boca Raton hiring guide lists questions to ask.

How much does mold inspection cost in Boca Raton?

National HomeAdvisor figures put mold inspection or assessment at $303–$1,075 (average about $689) and mold remediation at $1,223–$3,758 (average about $2,369). There are no Boca Raton-specific prices, so get written quotes. See the cost guide and estimate tool.

Can the same company test and remove mold in Boca Raton?

No. In Florida, the company that assesses your mold can't also remove it on the same job, so get assessment and remediation quotes from different licensed firms.
