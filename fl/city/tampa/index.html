Frequently asked questions

How do I check a mold pro's licence in Tampa?

Look the name up in the FL DBPR records and ask for the licence number in writing. Each listing here shows the licence numbers from the agency's records. The Tampa hiring guide lists questions to ask.

How much does mold inspection cost in Tampa?

National HomeAdvisor figures put mold inspection or assessment at $303–$1,075 (average about $689) and mold remediation at $1,223–$3,758 (average about $2,369). There are no Tampa-specific prices, so get written quotes. See the cost guide and estimate tool.

Can the same company test and remove mold in Tampa?

No. In Florida, the company that assesses your mold can't also remove it on the same job, so get assessment and remediation quotes from different licensed firms.
