How do I hire a licensed mold pro in Hernando County, FL?

Look the name up in the DBPR records, ask for the licence number in writing, make sure the licence type matches the job, and get proof of insurance and a written scope before work starts.

  1. Look the name up in the DBPR records (Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation). Our copy was retrieved 2026-10-12, so check that the licence is still current.
  2. Ask for the licence number in writing and make sure the licence type matches the job: assessor for testing and a protocol, remediator for removal.
  3. Use separate, independent firms for testing and for remediation (required by Florida law).
  4. Ask for proof of insurance and a written scope before work starts.
