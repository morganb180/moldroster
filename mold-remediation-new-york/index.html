Mold remediation in New York: licences, rules and who to hire

Reviewed by Mold Roster staff · Last updated November 1, 2026

Quick answer: New York licenses mold assessors and remediators through the NYS Department of Labor under Labor Law Article 32. A licence is needed for mold projects over 10 square feet. The assessor writes a work analysis, a different licensed company does the removal, and a post-remediation assessment ends in a clearance report.

Who needs a New York mold licence?

Anyone who performs or advertises mold assessment or remediation on a project over 10 square feet needs a NYS DOL licence (Labor Law §§ 930–931).

  • Applicants must be 18 or older, complete approved coursework and carry at least $50,000 in liability insurance (§ 932).
  • Licences last 2 years, and renewal needs continuing education (§ 934).
  • Exempt: a residential owner working on their own property; the owner of a building with 4 or fewer units; and owners, managing agents or employees working on commercial property or buildings over 4 units, unless doing business for the public (§ 933).
  • Routine cleaning isn't a mold project (§ 930).
  • Fines run up to $2,000 for a first violation and up to $10,000 for later ones (§ 937).

Can the same company assess and remove mold in New York?

No. A licensee may not do both the assessment and the remediation on the same property, or own an interest in a company that does the other half (§ 936).

Hire an independent assessor first. See assessment vs remediation.

What paperwork should I get?

The assessor's work analysis and the remediator's work plan before work starts, then a post-remediation assessment ending in a passed clearance report or a final status report.

  • The plan covers rooms and areas, quantities, methods, protective equipment, clearance criteria, a cost estimate, a time frame and the underlying moisture source (§ 945).
  • The remediator's work plan goes to the client, and a copy stays on the job site (§ 935).
  • After the work: a post-remediation assessment that ends in a passed clearance report, or a final status report (§ 947).

Do New York home inspectors need a mold licence?

Only if their reports assess mold. NYS DOL says home inspectors need a Mold Assessor licence for that work.

How many licensed mold businesses are in New York?

Our New York directory has 1,540 NYS DOL-licensed businesses from data.ny.gov, retrieved October 12, 2026. DOL publishes businesses, not individual workers.

  • 802 hold an assessment licence and 946 hold a remediation licence.
  • Listings have an address of record in 52 of New York's 62 counties.

How do I check a New York mold licence?

Use the licensed mold contractors search on the NYS DOL Mold Program page, and ask for the licence number in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a licence to remove mold in my own New York home?

No. Labor Law § 933 exempts a residential owner doing the work on their own property.

What size of mold job needs a licensed pro in New York?

Labor Law Article 32 applies to mold projects over 10 square feet; routine cleaning is excluded.

How long is a New York mold licence valid?

Two years. Renewal requires continuing education (Labor Law § 934).
