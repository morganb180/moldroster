Texas licenses mold assessment and remediation companies and the individual consultants and contractors who run projects. TDLR's public files give company addresses and phones, but only the name and county for individual licensees, so individuals are listed by county.

## What are Texas' mold licensing rules?

A TDLR licence is generally required to assess or remediate mold when the affected area is 25 contiguous square feet or more.

* A TDLR licence is generally required to assess or remediate mold when the affected area is 25 contiguous square feet or more.
* The same licensee may not do both the assessment and the remediation on the same project.
* Mold assessment consultants write the remediation protocol; licensed remediation contractors follow it, and the assessor does the post-remediation check.

Summary of 16 Texas Administrative Code Chapter 78 (Texas Mold Assessment and Remediation Rules). See the agency: [Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/mold/moldrules.htm). Plain-language summary, not legal advice; confirm details with the agency.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a licensed mold pro in Texas?**

A TDLR licence is generally required to assess or remediate mold when the affected area is 25 contiguous square feet or more.

**How many licensed mold pros are in Texas?**

Mold Roster lists 2,357 TDLR-licensed listings in Texas: 1,140 with an assessment licence and 1,290 with a remediation licence (records retrieved 2026-10-12).

**Can the same company test and remove the mold?**

The same licensee may not do both the assessment and the remediation on the same project.

**How much does mold remediation cost?**

A national cost guide puts typical remediation at $1,223-$3,757 (average about $2,368) and inspections at $303-$1,075. Get written quotes.
